## Practical Legal Guidance. Thoughtful Representation. Trusted Advocacy.

Legal challenges rarely fit neatly into one category. Whether you're defending your rights after a criminal charge, protecting your driving record, pursuing an appeal, or planning for your family's future, you deserve clear advice and experienced representation.

At **Walchesky Law**, we believe every client should understand their options, know what to expect, and have an attorney who is prepared to advocate for their best interests. We take the time to listen, develop a strategy tailored to your circumstances, and guide you through each step of the legal process with honesty, professionalism, and personal attention.

## Traffic Law

Even a routine traffic citation can have lasting consequences for your insurance, driving privileges, and employment. We represent drivers facing a wide range of traffic violations throughout the Charlotte metro area, providing straightforward advice and efficient representation designed to protect your record whenever possible.

Many traffic matters are handled on a flat-fee basis, allowing clients to understand both the process and the cost from the beginning.

## Appeals & Post-Conviction Relief

Not every legal battle ends when a trial concludes. Appeals and post-conviction proceedings require a different level of legal analysis, focused on identifying significant legal errors and presenting persuasive arguments before appellate courts.

With decades of experience in both state and federal appellate courts, **Walchesky Law** provides thoughtful appellate advocacy, honest case evaluations, and practical guidance for clients seeking to challenge convictions, sentencing decisions, or other legal errors.

Whether you are considering a direct appeal, post-conviction relief, or need appellate consultation during ongoing litigation, we provide strategic representation tailored to your circumstances.
